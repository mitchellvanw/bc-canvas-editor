# Variant 6 — Quiet sheet (round 3, derivative of 5)

## Style description

The drafting sheet calmed down. Keeps variant 5's DNA — warm paper with a faint
grid, ink title block, Archivo / Source Serif 4 / Plex Mono, sticky hues with
same-hue ink borders — but interprets "still a bit rough" as *too busy*: the
grid fades and widens (32px), the paper warms toward cream, panels get a soft
radius, more padding, and a whisper of shadow, and every decorative device is
asked to justify itself.

## Design decisions (deltas from 5)

- **Hints removed.** The italic teaching lines belong to the empty state (already
  parked in the map's "Not yet specified"), not to a filled canvas.
- **One message shape.** The cmd/qry/evt shape play (block / pill / arrow) is
  dropped; all messages are uniform rounded chips distinguished by fill color and
  glyph (▶ ? ◆). Craft over cleverness; long names wrap safely.
- **Section labels get a colored underline** in the section's hue instead of the
  colored square + full-width body rule — one device instead of two.
- **Collaborators lose their pink sticky fill**: name in collaborator-pink ink
  with a pink underline. The relationship badge loses its border and floats
  right as quiet mono. Lane = typographic unit, not a box in a box.
- **Terms as highlighter strokes** (yellow gradient under the term) instead of
  bordered yellow stickies — the highlight reads as marking, not as a widget.
- **Tinted section backgrounds dropped** (language/decisions panels are plain);
  the palette lives only on content rows and the label underlines.
- **Footer legend compressed** to one swatch line beside the attribution.

## Requirements mapping

Same canonical grid, content, palette assignments, and CC BY 4.0 attribution as
variant 5; classification stays in the title block.

## Trade-offs

- **Gains:** calm at density; the roughness complaints most likely to apply
  (visual noise, competing devices, shape gimmicks) are all addressed; easiest
  base for hover/edit affordances because the surface is quiet.
- **Sacrifices:** less distinctive than 5 — the drafting character survives only
  in the paper, grid, and title block; dropping the shapes loses a genuinely
  informative channel; collaborator lanes are subtler to scan.
