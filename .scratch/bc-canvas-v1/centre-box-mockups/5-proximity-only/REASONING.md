# 5 · Proximity only

**Style.** Nothing is drawn. The two centre panels pull together — an 8px gap
against the canvas's 18px everywhere else — and gestalt does the rest.

**Design decisions.** The quietest possible mechanism: grouping expressed
purely through the existing spacing system. The pair reads as a couple the way
tight kerning reads as a word.

**Requirements mapping.** Gray line gone, nothing added. Whether it "sets apart
subtly" enough is exactly what this mockup tests — it is the lower bound of
the exploration.

**Trade-offs.** Gains: absolute zero ink; no new idiom; free at every size.
Sacrifices: the weakest signal — the horizontal extent of the group is
unmarked, so nothing says the pair is *special*, only that it is *close*;
in the two-column responsive tier (pair side by side) an 8px horizontal gap
may just look like an alignment bug.
