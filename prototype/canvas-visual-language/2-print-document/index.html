Order Fulfillment

Business decisions
No partial shipments

An order ships complete or not at all.

Cost-first carrier selection

The cheapest carrier that meets the delivery promise wins.

Hazardous goods go ground

Never booked onto air freight, whatever the promise.

Assumptions
  • Warehouse stock counts are accurate within the hour.
  • A single warehouse fulfills any given order.
Verification metrics
  • Time from payment to dispatch under 4 hours.
  • Fewer than 1% of shipments re-booked with a second carrier.
