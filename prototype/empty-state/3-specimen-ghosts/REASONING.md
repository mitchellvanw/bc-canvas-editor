# Variant 3 — Specimen ghosts

**Bet:** show, don't tell — structured sections teach by a faint worked example.
Each empty structured section renders one specimen from the Order-Fulfillment
micro-canvas (a lane with typed chips, a term, a decision…) at half opacity
with a small dashed `example` tag. Prose fields keep question placeholders (a
ghost paragraph would read as real text). This is the round's answer to the
seeding question: seed nothing real, show everything as ghost — the {} drawer
proves the document stays empty until the user acts.

- **Disappearance:** a section's specimen dissolves at its first real content;
  clicking a specimen starts a real entry in its place; a "hide examples"
  toggle in the footer clears the whole layer at once.
- **Seeding:** ghost-only — nothing to delete before starting, nothing fake in
  an export.
- **Copy budget:** none — the example *is* the copy; message-type glyphs,
  relationship badges and term/definition shape are demonstrated rather than
  described.
- **Gains:** the only variant that shows what a *finished* section looks like —
  chips, colors, lane anatomy — before the user commits to anything.
- **Sacrifices:** the sheet never looks truly blank; half-opacity content risks
  being read as real (or as something that must be deleted); the example's
  domain (fulfillment) may anchor users modeling something else.
