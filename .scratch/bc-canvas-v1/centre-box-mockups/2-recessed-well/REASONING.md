# 2 · Recessed well

**Style.** The centre pair sits in a shallow depression pressed into the paper —
an inset shadow strongest along the top edge (light comes from above, matching
the panels' outward drop shadow) over a 2% wash.

**Design decisions.** Every other sheet on the canvas floats *above* the paper;
the one grouped region is the one place the paper *recedes*. Depth is the
differentiator, so the region needs no contour of its own.

**Requirements mapping.** No line — the boundary is a shadow gradient. Subtle:
at these alphas the well is barely-there until panels cross it. The pair reads
as "held" by the well.

**Trade-offs.** Gains: physical, quiet, directionally consistent with the
existing elevation language. Sacrifices: inset shadows render slightly
differently across browsers/zoom; the depth cue weakens on very tall centre
columns where the middle of the well is all wash and no shadow.
